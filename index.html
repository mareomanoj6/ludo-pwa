Rules of the Game

🎲
Exit Yard on a 6

Tokens remain in the base yard until a 6 is rolled. Rolling a 6 also grants an immediate bonus turn.

🛑
Three Sixes Rule

If a player or AI rolls 2 sixes, and then rolls a 3rd six, their move is skipped and their turn ends immediately.

⭐
Safe Cells

Cells marked with a star symbol and starting colored squares are safe zones. Pieces cannot be captured while resting on safe cells.

⚔️
Capturing Opponents

Landing on an opponent's piece on an unsafe cell captures it and sends it back to its yard. You receive a bonus roll!

🛡️
No Blockades

Tokens of the same color can share cells without blocking anyone from passing through.

🏆
Exact Roll to Finish

Tokens navigate around the perimeter, enter their private colored home column, and require an exact roll to enter the Center Finish.
