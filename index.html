Rules of the Game

🎲
Entering the Board & Bonus Turns

Tokens remain in the base yard until a 6 is rolled (or 1/6 if configured). Rolling a 6 grants an immediate extra roll. Capturing an opponent or reaching Home also grants a bonus roll (bonuses do not stack infinitely).

🛑
Three Sixes Rule

If a player rolls three 6s in a row, the 3rd roll is nullified and the turn immediately ends. Moves made on the first two 6s are preserved.

⭐
Safe Zones & Coexistence

The 4 colored starting squares and the 4 star-marked cells are safe zones. Opponents cannot be captured on safe cells and can safely coexist simultaneously.

⚔️
Capturing Opponents

Landing on an opponent's token on any regular track cell captures it and sends it back to its starting yard. You receive 1 bonus roll!

🛡️
Blockades / Doubling (Configurable)

When Blockades are enabled in Settings, 2 tokens of the same color on a square form an impassable barrier that opponents cannot pass or land on.

🏆
Home Column & Exact Finish

Tokens must complete a full clockwise circuit before turning into their colored home stretch. Reaching the center Home finish requires an exact dice roll.

🥇
Podium Rankings & Auto-Save

When a player finishes, play continues for 2nd and 3rd place! Game progress is automatically saved to your device so you can resume anytime.
